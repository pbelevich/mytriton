02 · Why the first attempt was wrong

Python is 40× larger than a small GPU kernel

Wrong measurement

Public launches return deep copies of expression IR and SSA. At 1024³ the wrapper needed roughly 1.9–2.0 ms.

Correct measurement

The actual GPU kernel needed roughly 0.04–0.07 ms. CUDA events exclude compilation, IR copies, DLPack conversion and synchronization.

Timing the wrapper selected an eight-warp 4096³ kernel that delivered only 140.10 TFLOP/s. Timing cached RawKernel execution selected the expected four-warp static pipeline.

07 · Honest limits

What Version 28 deliberately does not solve

Cold cost

Generating and NVRTC-compiling five large kernels can take minutes. Winner records are process-local and candidate pruning is absent.

Kernel ceiling

Autotune can choose only what exists. The winner still uses 213 registers/thread and 12.5% theoretical occupancy.

Next: create better candidates—lower register pressure, improved warp scheduling, persistent/grouped tiles—and make the public cached launch path cheaper.
