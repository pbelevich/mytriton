1. The new hierarchy

Physical work per warp

A warp owns a 64×64 output tile. SM80 decomposes its K=32 slice into:

M atoms64 / 16 = 4
N atoms64 / 8 = 8
K atoms32 / 16 = 2
MMA instructions64

Each lane holds 128 FP32 accumulator values. This creates reuse, but also a long dependency graph and high register pressure.
