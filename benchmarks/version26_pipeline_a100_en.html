02 · THREE SHARED STAGES

A rotating ring breaks the chain.

One stage is consumed, one is ready, and one is being filled. Indices rotate modulo three.

Stage 0

consume K0
refill K3

A barrier prevents early overwrite.

Stage 1

prefetched K1
consume K1

wait_group 1 publishes it.

Stage 2

cp.async K2
consume K2

The copy engine advances independently.

prologue: issue K0 → issue K1 → wait_group 1 → barrier loop t: issue K(t+2) ║ ldmatrix Kt → mma.sync Kt wait_group 1 → barrier → rotate stage
03 · FAST AND SAFE

Asynchronous does not mean unsafe.

A 16-byte copy needs alignment. Odd leading dimensions use a scalar zero-fill producer under the same synchronization protocol.

Aligned complete vector

One instruction moves a complete vector without temporary value registers.

Tail or misaligned vector

Valid elements are copied; the remainder is zero. No out-of-bounds read occurs.
